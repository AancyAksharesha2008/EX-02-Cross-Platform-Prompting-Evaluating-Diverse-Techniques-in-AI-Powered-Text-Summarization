# EX-02-Cross-Platform-Prompting-Evaluating-Diverse-Techniques-in-AI-Powered-Text-Summarization

## AIM
To evaluate and compare the effectiveness of prompting techniques (zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought, role-based) across different AI platforms (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot) in a specific task: text summarization.

## Scenario:
You are part of a content curation team for an educational platform that delivers quick summaries of research papers to undergraduate students. Your task is to summarize a 500-word technical article on "The Basics of Blockchain Technology" using multiple AI platforms and prompting strategies.

Your goal is to determine which combination of prompting technique + platform provides the best summary in terms of:

Accuracy

Coherence

Simplicity

Speed

User experience

## Algorithm

Step 1: Select the Article
1.1 Choose a 500-word technical article on “The Basics of Blockchain Technology”.

1.2 Identify the main concepts, such as blocks, transactions, decentralization, and security.

Step 2: Select AI Platforms

2.1 Choose AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Microsoft Copilot.

2.2 Use the same article on all platforms for fair comparison.

Step 3: Apply Prompting Techniques

3.1 Zero-Shot Prompting: Ask the AI to summarize the article without examples.

3.2 Few-Shot Prompting: Provide sample summaries to guide the AI's output.

3.3 Chain-of-Thought Prompting: Ask the AI to identify key concepts and organize the information before producing the summary.

3.4 Role-Based Prompting: Assign the AI the role of an educational content writer for undergraduate students.

Step 4: Generate Summaries

4.1 Enter the prompts into each AI platform.

4.2 Generate summaries using all four prompting techniques.

4.3 Record the time taken and observe the quality of each summary.

Step 5: Evaluate the Results

5.1 Check accuracy and whether important concepts are included.

5.2 Evaluate coherence and logical flow.

5.3 Check simplicity and readability for undergraduate students.

5.4 Compare response speed and overall user experience.

Step 6: Compare and Identify the Best Technique

6.1 Compare the summaries generated by different platforms.

6.2 Identify the platform and prompting technique that provide the clearest, most accurate, and concise summary.

6.3 Record the final observations.
## Result
The experiment demonstrates how different prompting techniques can influence the quality of AI-generated text summaries. Zero-shot prompting provides quick summaries, while few-shot prompting helps guide the output format. Chain-of-thought-style instructions can encourage organized results, and role-based prompting helps tailor summaries to undergraduate students.

The effectiveness of each technique may vary across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Microsoft Copilot. The best combination should be selected based on accuracy, coherence, simplicity, speed, and user experience.
